# 11. Keep: a shortlist in this browser, shareable as a link

- **Status:** accepted, 2026-11-20
- **Deciders:** Sohab Sk

## Context

The site is good at the next hop and had nowhere to land. Hopping from show to show, a visitor collects candidates in their head and loses them, and a session that ends with nothing to show for it is the one that leaves for good. The path discussion in #4 put a shortlist first.

## Decision

- **Keep** on a show's hero, next to Where to watch. Tapped, it reads **Kept**, and a polite status line says what happened (the label changes, so it isn't also a pressed toggle).
- The list lives in **localStorage** (`slm:kept`, slugs, newest first, 100 at most): a shortlist that vanished with the tab would be no use. No account, nothing sent anywhere; the About page says so.
- A **Kept · N** pill in the header appears once there's one, counted before first paint, and kept in step across tabs and Back.
- **/kept/** draws the list from the search index, so status and hours are this week's, and a show that leaves the catalogue drops out. Each show has Remove; focus stays in the list.
- **Share this list** uses the phone's share sheet, else copies `/kept/?s=slug,slug`. A shared link shows its shows with **Keep all**, which adds them ahead of yours. Unknown slugs are ignored. The page is `noindex`.

## Consequences

- Lists stay on one browser. Syncing across devices would need accounts and a store (not v1).
- Sharing is the one way a list leaves the browser, and it's the visitor's choice; the link holds only show slugs.
- Tile styles moved to global.css, and the browser-built tile to `src/lib/tile.ts`, now shared by home search and /kept/.
