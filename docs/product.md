# Shows Like Map: product brief

**One line:** Find your next show, one you'll actually finish.

## The problem

You finish a show you loved and want another like it. The usual answers (listicles, Reddit threads, "more like this" rows, chatbots) tell you what's *similar*, but not what you're signing up for:

- Is it **finished**, still running, or **cancelled on a cliffhanger**?
- How long is it: a **6-hour weekend** or a **150-hour commitment**?
- Where does it stream **in India**?
- And Indian web series are badly covered by Western sites.

## Who it's for

People who watch series on streaming services, starting from a show they already loved: first a strong global catalogue, then Indian web series as the next priority. They come from search ("shows like Dark") or share links.

## What it does

1. **Start from a show you loved** (search or a starter grid).
2. **See the shows a fan of it would enjoy next**, each with a one-line reason ("Time-loop mystery across generations of one small town").
3. **Every card answers the commitment question:** finished, ongoing or cancelled; total hours to finish; seasons; the platform it's on.
4. **Filter to what you'll actually finish:** finished only, under N hours, on my platforms, language.
5. **Drill down:** tap a show to go deeper, hold to peek at its matches, and see the path you took on a small map.

## Edges

- **Commitment-first:** hours to finish and finished/cancelled status on every card.
- **Global first, India next:** a strong global catalogue first; then Indian web series as first-class, with Indian shows getting Indian picks.
- **Good matches:** AI-curated lists validated against real data (an eval scored 0.89 top-5 precision, versus 0.25 for TMDB's own recommendations).
- **Fun to explore:** drill-down, peek and path map, not a static list.
- **Open:** the data is open (TVmaze CC BY-SA, Wikidata CC0) and the "shows like X" graph is published as an open dataset.
- **Free:** no ads, no login, no tracking, instant static pages.

## Non-goals (v1)

- Accounts, watchlists, ratings or reviews by users.
- Live per-country streaming availability (no open source has it; we link out to JustWatch).
- Movies.
- Any revenue (ads, affiliate links).

## Success looks like

- A visitor from "shows like X" search finds a match they start watching.
- Show pages rank for "shows like <show>" searches.
- Match quality holds up on lesser-known Indian shows, not just famous ones.
